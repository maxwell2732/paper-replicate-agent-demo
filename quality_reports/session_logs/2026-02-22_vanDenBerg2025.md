## Goal

Replicate Table 1, Panel A (OLS estimates) from van den Berg, von Hinke & Wang (2025, PNAS) for the 3 outcomes available in our UKB extract (edu_years, height, t2dm).

---

## Approach

Standard RD/event-study OLS with clustered SEs. The key quasi-experiment: UK confectionery derationing April–August 1949 created exogenous prenatal sugar exposure for those born April 1949 – May 1950 (E=1).

**Key specification:**
```
y ~ E + male + month_f + time_c + I(time_c * E)
```
where `time_c` = linear calendar time centered at April 1949, `month_f` = 11 birth-month dummies (Jan reference), SEs clustered at year-month.

---

## Key Decisions

1. **Sample window:** April 1947 – May 1952 (yearmo = −153 to −92). The plan had a typo (−165 instead of −153 for April 1947); corrected in script.
2. **Ethnicity filter:** Self-reported White British (n_21000=1001) as proxy for genetic British ancestry (field 22006 not available). Approximation noted in validation report.
3. **Adoption/multiple-birth filter:** Exclude n_1767==1 (adopted) and n_1777==1 (multiple birth); keep −1 (prefer not to answer) and NA as non-excluded.
4. **T2DM:** Binary = 1 if ts_130708_0_0 is non-missing (any recorded first-occurrence date). NA = no T2DM.
5. **Cluster SEs:** vcovCL with type="HC1" at dob_yearmo level.

## Placebo Falsification Results (Phase 9, added 2026-02-22)

Placebo windows (same 14-month width): pre = Apr 1947–May 1948 (−153 to −140), post = Apr 1951–May 1952 (−105 to −92).

| Outcome | Window | α₁ | SE | p-value | Significant? |
|---------|--------|-----|-----|---------|-------------|
| edu_years | Pre-placebo | −0.085 | 0.089 | 0.340 | No |
| edu_years | Post-placebo | −0.145 | 0.061 | **0.017** | Yes* |
| height | Pre-placebo | −0.057 | 0.100 | 0.567 | No |
| height | Post-placebo | −0.047 | 0.123 | 0.703 | No |
| t2dm | Pre-placebo | −0.0015 | 0.0035 | 0.655 | No |
| t2dm | Post-placebo | −0.0017 | 0.0033 | 0.617 | No |

**Note:** Post-placebo for edu_years is significant at 5% (p=0.017). Coefficient is negative (−0.145) while real effect is positive (+0.111), suggesting opposite-sign spurious correlation rather than real treatment. Height and t2dm placebos cleanly non-significant.
